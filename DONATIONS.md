## How mining support works

The app uses **XMRig** with the **MoneroOcean** pool. When a volunteer explicitly starts mining, pool rewards are directed to the address above; **the volunteer does not receive those rewards**. XMRig has a developer donation, and the pool may apply its normal fees. Mining rates and payouts vary.

Mining uses your **hardware and electricity** and can cost more to run than the donation is worth. The app has visible controls and an immediate STOP button for ordinary operation; read [SAFETY.md](docs/SAFETY.md) before using it.

**Prefer to donate directly?** A Monero transfer to the public address above avoids the electricity and hardware costs of mining. There's no obligation to contribute either way.
